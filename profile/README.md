# Lead Friendly

Lead Friendly is an AI phone agent with a built-in CRM for small businesses.

It answers your business line 24/7 as your business, texts back missed calls, books appointments on your calendar, and logs every call, text and email on the contact's timeline with recordings, transcripts and summaries. Paste your website and the agent learns your services and hours. The CRM, the AI voice agents and the telephony are on one plan, so there is one bill and one contact record.

Built for compliance from day one. The agent says it is an AI, outbound calls respect quiet hours in the called person's time zone, and do-not-call and STOP requests are honored automatically. Every outbound channel goes through a hard compliance gate (KYC, AUP, quarantine, E911, AI-window, velocity, PEWC, DNC obligations) before a single byte leaves the platform. STOP/HELP, CAN-SPAM postal address + one-click unsubscribe, two-party recording rules — all wired in.

For independent used-car dealers, Lead Friendly is the phone and follow-up layer on top of the DMS and CRM the store already runs. The store keeps its DMS, its CRM, its lenders and its desk.

Plans are $49, $99 and $199 a month with published pricing. The free trial is 7 days with 30 AI voice minutes, no card needed.

Developers: the hosted MCP servers are in [leadfriendly/mcp](https://github.com/leadfriendly/mcp).
